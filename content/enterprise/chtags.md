**Vantage Chtags** is the enterprise build of Vantage for Club House
Tags. It bundles the chtags data model — tags, batches, and golf
courses — together with built-in actions such as tag printing, so the
admin app works against the live database out of the box.

Install it like any Mac app: download, open the disk image, and drag
**Vantage Chtags** into Applications. It lives alongside a standard
Vantage install and keeps its own settings.
